# THE KEPT
**Grim Reel Stories · a 5-part fictional horror series**

**Format:** vertical 9:16, 2D silhouette style, about 65–75 s per part.
**Voice:** Unmarked lines are the NARRATOR, the calm voice inside Ansel's head. It speaks in the second person and gets colder every part. `[VOICE: …]` marks another speaker. Ansel's spoken lines (`[VOICE: Ansel]`) use the narrator's actor, flatter and slightly off-mic, so the "Took?" / "Takes." exchange is easy to catch by ear.
**Fiction notice:** Ansel Crane, the town of Wrenfield, the Lantern Diner and every person in this story are invented. No real killer, case or victim is depicted.
**His logic:** His mother left when he was nine and left soup on the stove. As long as the soup lasted, it felt like she was still there. Everyone leaves him. He comes to believe that someone he has "kept" (eaten, which is never shown or described) can never leave.
**Why not all of them (his rule):** Only the kind ones get kept. Anyone who is afraid of him "has to go", because fear isn't love. After the 27th person he keeps, keeping stops filling the emptiness, so he stops keeping anyone.
**Running counter (same top corner, every part):** P1 ends `KILLED 1 · EATEN 0` · P2 ends `9 · 5` · P3 ends `26 · 15` · P4 ends `51 · 27` · P5 ends `51 · 27`, then `KEPT: 0`.
**Edit rule:** Every death is a hard cut, a sound or an aftermath: a freezer hum, a set table, a missing poster, an empty apron hook. There is no violence, cooking or eating in the picture or the voice-over, and no method is shown. Every victim gets one human detail. No victim's silhouette ever sits at his table.
